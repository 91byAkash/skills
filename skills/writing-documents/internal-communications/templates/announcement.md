# Announcement (change, launch, policy, org)

Answer the six questions readers have, in this order. Check it with `comms_check.py --type announcement`.

```
Subject/first line: <What is changing> on <date>, and what it means for you

What: <one sentence>
Why: <the problem it solves or the goal>
Who is affected: <everyone / teams X and Y / only people who …>
When: <absolute date(s) and zone if time matters; phases>
What you need to do: <action and deadline, or "nothing, it's automatic">
Questions: <#channel / person / office hours link>
```

Rules:
- If the reader needs to act, the action and deadline go in the first two lines, not paragraph four.
- Org changes: tell the people directly affected before the broad post. Lead with what stays the same for most people.
- Don't oversell. "We're moving to X because Y" beats "We're thrilled to share an exciting new chapter".
- Plan a reminder close to the deadline, and say you'll send one.
