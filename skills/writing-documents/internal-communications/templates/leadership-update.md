# Leadership update

For execs with five minutes and many reports. Put the headline and the asks first, then the detail. Check it with `comms_check.py --type leadership --channel email`.

```
Subject: <Area>: <the headline in a few words> (<date>)

TL;DR
- <most important point: status or result>
- <second>
- <third, at most>

Decisions needed
1. <decision>, by <date>. Options: A <cost/benefit>, B <cost/benefit>. Recommendation: <A>, because <reason>.
(or: "No decisions needed this week.")

Detail
<short sections, each opening with its conclusion; numbers vs plan>

Risks
- <risk>: likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner
```

Rules:
- Three bullets at most in the TL;DR. If a reader stops there, they still know what matters.
- Every decision comes with options, a recommendation and a deadline. Don't send open-ended "thoughts?".
- Bad news goes in the TL;DR, not the appendix. Give the number, the cause and the plan.
- Link to detail docs rather than pasting them in.
- Spell out team-internal acronyms and code names.
