# FAQ round-up

Collect the questions that confuse many people across the company this week, and answer each one briefly from official sources. Check it with `comms_check.py --type faq`.

## Format

```
- *Question*: <one sentence, phrased the way people actually asked it>
- *Answer*: <1-2 sentences> (<link to the authoritative source>)
```

Group the questions under small headers (Company, Product, People/benefits, Tools) once you have more than 8.

## Picking questions
- Prefer questions that many people asked: lots of reactions or "+1" replies, repeats across channels, questions raised at all-hands or in AMA docs.
- Cover the whole company, not just the requester's team.
- Good areas: recent corporate events (funding, new executives, reorgs), upcoming launches, hiring, changes to strategy or focus, benefits and policy changes, office and tool changes.
- Merge duplicates into one canonical wording.

## Answer rules
- Base every answer on an official communication (announcement, policy doc, exec email) and link it.
- If the answer isn't confirmed, say so plainly: "Not decided yet; leadership expects to confirm by 30 Oct." Never present a guess as fact.
- If a question needs an executive or official answer (compensation, legal, layoffs, M&A), don't answer it. List it under **Needs an official answer** with who should respond.
- Keep the tone professional but approachable: no corporate hedging, no jokes about sensitive topics.
- Never include who asked, unless it was asked publicly and they'd expect credit.

Needs an official answer
