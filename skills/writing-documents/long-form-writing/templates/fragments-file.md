# Gate debt (working title)

Every team added one check. Nobody removed one.

---

Deploy time went from 6 to 14 minutes in a year, and no single change took more than 90 seconds.

- 9 gates added
- 0 retired
- 2 that caught nothing in 12 months

---

> "We can't remove it, we don't know who owns it."  (platform on-call, overheard)

That sentence is the whole problem.

---

Something about how checks are like smoke alarms: cheap to add, nobody tests them, and you only notice when one shrieks at 3 a.m. Work this out later.

---

**Gate debt**: checks that outlived the reason they were added.
