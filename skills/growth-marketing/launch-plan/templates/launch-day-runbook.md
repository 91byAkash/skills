## Escalation
- Signup, login or payment broken → page on-call, pause paid and email sends, post holding reply.
- Negative thread gaining traction → owner replies within 30 minutes with facts, no defensiveness.
- Outage > 15 minutes → status page update, roll back if a fix is not certain.
