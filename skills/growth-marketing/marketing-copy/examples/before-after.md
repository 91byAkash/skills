# Before/after rewrites (annotated)

## 1. SaaS hero (invoicing tool for freelancers)

Before
> Unlock the power of seamless invoicing — reimagined for modern freelancers.
> Our all-in-one solution empowers you to streamline your workflow. Get started

Problems: two banned phrases, capped words stacked, em dash in a headline, fails "Now you can…", fails the swap test, weak CTA.

After
> Get paid 9 days sooner, without chasing clients
> Invoices go out the moment you log time, and polite reminders follow up for you. 6,200 freelancers average 9 days faster payment in their first quarter.
> [Send my first invoice] · Free for your first 5 clients

Why: outcome + specific number in the headline; mechanism in the subhead; proof number; first-person CTA naming the action; risk reducer.

## 2. Feature section (analytics product)

Before
> **Robust Dashboards.** Our intuitive, state-of-the-art dashboards deliver actionable insights, enabling data-driven decisions.

After
> **See which campaign paid for itself, every Monday.** A weekly report ranks campaigns by revenue per dollar, so you can cut the losers before next week's spend. "We moved 30% of budget in the first month." Priya N., Growth Lead, a 40-person DTC brand

Why: outcome headline, how it works in one sentence, attributed proof; removed a stack of four "limit" words (robust, intuitive, state-of-the-art, actionable insights).

## 3. Pricing tier blurbs

Before
> Starter: For individuals. Pro: For professionals. Business: For businesses.

After
> Starter, $19: for solo consultants sending up to 20 invoices a month.
> Pro, $49 (most popular): for small studios with 2-10 people and recurring clients.
> Business, $129: for agencies that need approvals, multiple entities and SSO.

Why: each tier answers "is this for me?" with a size and a trigger feature.

## 4. CTA set (demo request page)

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Submit | Book my 20-minute demo | Action + what they get + time cost |
| Learn more | See a sample report | Concrete next step |
| Contact sales | Get pricing for 50+ seats | Matches the intent of large buyers |

## 5. Problem section (expense tool)

Before
> Managing expenses can be challenging for growing teams in today's fast-paced world.

After
> It's the 28th. You're missing 40 receipts, three people are on holiday, and the accountant wants the books by Friday.

Why: a specific scene in the customer's words replaces an era opener and a vague claim.
