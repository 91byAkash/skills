# Community strategy reference

Contents: 1 principles · 2 models · 3 playbooks · 4 platform choice · 5 channel architecture and rituals · 6 health metrics · 7 scaling roles · 8 outputs.

## 1. Principles

- **Shared identity first.** People join for the product and stay for the people and who they become (bootstrapped founders, self-hosting tinkerers, designers who care about craft). Write the identity down: "This is where [who] come to [become/do what]."
- **Value flows to members first**: knowledge or early access they can't get elsewhere, peer connections, status and recognition, real influence on the roadmap, career visibility.
- **Flywheel**: members join → get value → engage → create content and help others → that content attracts new members. Judge every decision by whether it speeds the loop.
- Before tactics, confirm: product/brand, platform, stage (pre-launch, 0-100, 100-1k, 1k+), primary goal (retention, activation, word of mouth, support deflection, feedback, revenue), ideal member and motivation, seed audience available, weekly hours available.

## 2. Models

| Model | Best when | Core loop | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support-driven | Many repetitive how-to questions; customers can help each other | Ask → peer answer → answer becomes searchable knowledge | Turning into a complaint channel; unanswered questions |
| Product-development | Engaged power users; fast-moving product | Feedback → visible change → credit to members | Ignoring feedback publicly |
| Education/enablement | Product success depends on skill | Learn → apply → share results | Becoming a course with no peer interaction |
| Founder-led | Early stage; founder has a voice and audience | Founder shares → members respond → founder involves them | Doesn't scale without systems |
| Identity/peer network | Strong shared profession or aspiration | Members connect, share wins, help each other | Product feels bolted on |

Benchmark for community-led growth at scale: one well-known productivity tool built a program of 300+ ambassadors, over a million template downloads, and about a quarter of new users arriving via community referrals.

## 3. Playbooks

**Launch from zero**
1. Recruit 20-50 founding members personally (engaged users, beta testers, fans). Don't open publicly until there's baseline activity.
2. Write guidelines that describe what great participation looks like, not just rules.
3. Seed 5-10 posts that model the behavior you want (questions, wins, resources).
4. Do unscalable things: reply to every post, welcome everyone by name, host a weekly call.
5. Define the core weekly action and reward it publicly.

**Grow an existing community**
1. Find the leak: joined but never posted? posted once and vanished?
2. Build a new-member journey: pinned welcome, intro channel, personal DM, a clear "start here" path, first-week prompts.
3. Showcase member wins (projects, milestones, testimonials).
4. Run rituals: weekly "what are you working on?", monthly AMAs, seasonal challenges.
5. Invest in power users (often ~1% generate most of the value): recognition, early access, moderator roles, direct product input.

**Ambassador/advocate program**
1. Find people already recommending you (reviews, mentions, community answers).
2. Invite personally, saying why them.
3. Offer meaningful benefits: access, recognition, swag, revenue share, speaking slots.
4. Equip them: referral links, assets, talking points, a private channel.
5. Track referral traffic, signups and engagement; iterate (incentive mechanics in retention-and-referrals).

**Community-led support**
1. Turn top questions into a searchable knowledge base.
2. Recognize helpers (expert badges, leaderboards, shout-outs).
3. Close the loop: announce changes driven by feedback and credit the members.
4. Review sentiment weekly for confusion or churn signals.

## 4. Platform choice

| Platform type | Best for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Real-time chat servers | Developers, gaming, creators | Noisy, hard to search, onboarding friction |
| Workplace chat workspaces | B2B professional communities | Free-tier history limits; feels like work |
| Hosted community platforms | Creators, courses, paid communities | Little organic discovery; you drive traffic |
| Public forums on large networks | High-volume public discussion, search visibility | You don't own it; moderation is hard |
| Social-network groups | Consumer brands, older demographics | Declining organic reach; algorithm dependence |
| Self-hosted forum software | Long-form technical discussion, searchable archives | Slower pace; more effort per post |

## 5. Channel architecture and rituals

Start with few channels (5-7): welcome/start-here, introductions, general, help/questions, wins/showcase, feedback/ideas, announcements (staff-only). Add channels only when a topic overflows. Each channel gets a one-line purpose and posting guideline.

Ritual calendar example: Monday "goals for the week" thread; Wednesday office hours or help session; Friday wins thread; monthly AMA with an expert or the product team; quarterly challenge or virtual event.

New member journey: join → automated welcome with 3 first steps → personal DM within 24h → prompt to introduce with a template → day-3 nudge to ask a question → day-7 invite to a ritual.

## 6. Health metrics (weekly)

| Metric | Healthy signal |
|---|---|
| Active ratio (DAU/MAU) | > ~20% for most communities |
| New-member post rate | % of new members posting within 7 days, rising |
| Thread reply rate | % of posts with ≥ 1 reply; aim > 80% |
| Time to first reply | < 24 hours (ideally hours) |
| Member-created content | % of posts not by staff, rising |
| Lurkers | Members without a post in 30+ days, tracked by cohort |

Warning signs: most posts come from staff; questions unanswered > 24h; 5 people make 80%+ of activity; new members go silent after their intro.

Business link: retention and expansion of community members vs non-members, support tickets deflected, referrals and signups from the community, feedback items shipped.

## 7. Scaling roles

| Phase | Members | Role | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 0-100 | Architect | Relationships, culture, unscalable personal attention |
| Systems | 100-1,000 | Manager | Rituals, moderation, onboarding paths, norms that run without you |
| Scale | 1,000+ | Enabler | Ambassadors, sub-communities and member leaders; you design leverage |

## 8. Outputs

Community strategy doc (identity, platform, core loop, 90-day plan); channel architecture; new-member journey with DM templates; ritual calendar; ambassador program brief (criteria, benefits, outreach message, tracking); health audit (metrics, diagnosis, top 3 fixes). Be specific enough to act on today.
