# Interview guide (30 minutes)

Before: assumption we're trying to disprove: ______ · recording consent · incentive sent?

**Warm-up (3 min)**: role, team, what a normal week looks like.

**Before us (8 min)**
- Tell me about the moment you realized you needed something for [job]. What was happening?
- What were you doing before? What did that cost you?
- What else did you look at or try? Who else was involved?

**Deciding (7 min)**
- What almost stopped you from buying/switching?
- What made you decide? What did you have to convince others of?

**Using (7 min)**
- When did it first feel worth it? What happened?
- What's still annoying? What workaround have you built?
- If it disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead? What would you miss most?

**Ladder** any answer: "Why does that matter?" ×3-5 until you hit a business outcome or feeling.

**Close (2 min)**: "How would you describe us to a colleague?" · "Who else should we talk to?"

After (within 1 hour): top 3 quotes verbatim · triggers · alternatives · objections · surprises · did the assumption survive?
