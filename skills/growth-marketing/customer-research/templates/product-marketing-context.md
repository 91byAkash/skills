Other skills read this file before writing anything customer-facing. Mark each claim with where it came from (interview, review, sales call, assumption).

## 1. Who buys
**Best-fit accounts:** industry, size, stage, and the signal that they are ready.

**Roles in the purchase**
| Role (pays / decides / uses / blocks) | What they care about | Their main headache | What we promise them | How sure we are, and why |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Poor fits we should turn away:** who they are and why they churn or never convert.

## 2. The moment they start looking
- **Trigger:** the event that makes the status quo unacceptable.
- **The job:** "When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]." Add one line per secondary use case.
- **What the problem costs:** hours, money or risk, with a figure if we have one.
- **How it feels:** the frustration or fear, in a buyer's own words where possible.

## 3. What we are compared against
| Alternative | Kind (rival product / different approach / doing nothing) | Where it lets buyers down | What we do instead |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Why we win
- The two or three differences buyers actually notice, each with the mechanism behind it.
- Why that mechanism produces a better result.
- Reasons customers give for choosing us, quoted.

**Positioning in one sentence:** For [who] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit], unlike [alternative], because [proof].

## 5. What makes them hesitate
**Forces on the switch**
| Force | What we have heard | How marketing or onboarding responds |
|---|---|---|
| Frustration with the current way (push) | | amplify |
| Appeal of the new way (pull) | | amplify |
| Comfort of the current way (habit) | | reduce: migration help, defaults |
| Worry about the new way (anxiety) | | reduce: guarantees, proof |

**Objections**
| What they say | How we answer | Evidence we can show |
|---|---|---|

## 6. Product facts
One-line description · what it does, in two or three sentences · the category buyers would search under · product type, business model and pricing.
