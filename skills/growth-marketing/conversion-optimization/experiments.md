# Test Ideas by Surface

Use for the "A/B test first" section. Check traffic is enough first (analytics-and-experiments).

## Homepage
- Specific outcome headline vs category headline; social-proof headline ("Join 4,000 agencies…")
- Hero visual: product screenshot vs short loop vs interactive demo
- CTA: "Start free trial" vs "See it in action" vs "Get a demo"; CTA in nav
- Logos/case-study stat in hero vs below fold
- Section order: proof before features; benefit-led vs feature-led sections
- Sticky header with CTA; chat widget vs none

## Pricing page
- Annual default vs monthly default; savings shown as % vs currency
- 3 vs 4 visible tiers; "Most popular" badge on target plan; plan order (high→low anchoring)
- Enterprise price shown ("from $X") vs "Contact sales"
- Pricing calculator for usage-based plans
- FAQ placement; guarantee near CTA; trust badges; customer quote per plan
- Price endings ($99 vs $100) only with large traffic

## Demo request page
- Fields: 3 vs 6; calendar on the same page vs after submit
- "What happens next" block; length of call stated
- Routing small companies to self-serve; video of the demo as an alternative CTA

## Landing pages
- Exact ad-language headline vs rewritten; one CTA vs two
- Long vs short page (match awareness level); removing nav
- Price on page vs not; guarantee wording; testimonial type (video vs text)

## Feature pages
- Use-case framing vs capability framing; GIF vs static; inline "try it" CTA vs end CTA

## Blog / resources
- Inline contextual CTA vs end-of-post only; content upgrade (template) vs newsletter; exit-intent lead magnet

## Forms and signup
- Single vs split name; phone optional vs removed; multi-step vs single
- Social sign-in first vs email first; magic link vs password
- Email verification immediately vs deferred; "No credit card" line near button

## Onboarding
- Pre-filled templates/sample data vs empty state
- Checklist length (3 vs 6); one item pre-completed; progress bar vs none
- Role/goal question at start (personalized path) vs none
- Interactive tour vs tooltips vs none; skip option
- Founder welcome email vs product email; behavior-triggered vs scheduled emails
- Quick-start vs full setup path; human onboarding call offer for high-value accounts

## Popups
- Trigger: exit vs scroll 50% vs 45 s; incentive vs no incentive; headline angle; first-person CTA; slide-in vs modal

## Paywalls / upgrade
- Trigger timing (limit hit vs feature click vs day N); preview of locked feature vs description
- Trial length (7 vs 14); card up front vs not; annual vs monthly emphasis; value summary on trial-end screen

## Checkout and upsells
- Wallets above card form; guest checkout; order summary placement
- Upsell offer (A vs B), then price, then headline (one at a time); downsell on/off
- Order bump on/off (guardrail: base conversion)
