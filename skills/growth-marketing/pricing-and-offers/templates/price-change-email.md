# Price change email (existing customers)

Send 30-90 days before the effective date (longer for big increases or annual contracts). Plain, specific, no spin. Prepare support macros and a save path before sending.

**Subject options**
- Changes to your [Product] plan on [date]
- Your [Product] price is changing on [date], plus a way to keep today's rate

**Body**

Hi [first name],

On [effective date], the price of the [plan] plan will change from [old price] to [new price] per [period].

Why: since you joined, we've added [2-3 concrete improvements tied to value they've used, e.g. "bank sync for 4 more countries", "approval workflows"], and [honest reason: costs, investment in X].

What this means for you:
- Your current price stays until [date]. Nothing changes before then.
- [Soft landing] Want to keep today's price? Switch to annual before [date] and lock [old price] for 12 months: [link].
- [If relevant] If the new price doesn't fit, the [lighter plan] at [price] includes [what they actually use]: [link].

Questions? Reply to this email; a real person on our team reads every one.

Thanks for building with us,
[Name], [role]

**Follow-ups**
- Reminder 14 days before (same facts, same options).
- Day-of confirmation (receipt shows new price).
- Monitor: replies by sentiment, downgrade/cancel rate by cohort for two billing cycles.
